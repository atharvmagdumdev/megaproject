What is Vocally?

Vocally is an advanced Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) platform powered by state-of-the-art transformer models. We utilize a fine-tuned Wav2Vec2 base architecture capable of distinguishing 7 distinct emotional states in raw audio: Happy, Sad, Angry, Neutral, Fear, Disgust, and Surprise.


Rather than relying solely on text-based sentiment analysis, Vocally listens to the nuanced intonations, pitch variations, and speed of speech, bringing a much deeper layer of understanding to human communication.
