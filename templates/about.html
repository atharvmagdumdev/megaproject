About Us

While most technology focuses on the "what" of communication—the actual words being said or typed—we realized that it's often the "how" that contains the true meaning. Emotions shape the way people think, decide, and connect. Yet, in modern digital communication, those critical emotional layers are frequently lost.

Vocally isn't a massive corporate endeavor. It started as a collaborative project meant to explore how Speech Emotion Recognition (SER) models could accurately pick up on subtle vocal behaviors—from pitch and volume to speed and intonation.

We are Atharv, Arhant, Indrajeet, and Samarth — four students who set out to build something meaningful that bridges the gap between human emotion and artificial intelligence.

Our Purpose

We aim to connect human emotions with AI-driven insights, proving that understanding the sentiment behind the voice can lead to more empathetic and communicative technology.
